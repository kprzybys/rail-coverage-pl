# Data licenses

- **Measurements** (`trips/*/ping.csv`, `track.gpx`, `trip.json`, generated reports and figures):
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit: "rail-coverage-pl contributors".
- **Station coordinates** (`trips/*/stations.json`): from OpenStreetMap,
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).
- **Base station locations** (`trips/*/bts.json`): derived from the public register of radio
  permits published by Urząd Komunikacji Elektronicznej (bip.uke.gov.pl), with the
  "stan na" date recorded in each file.
- **Map tiles** on the website: OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and OpenRailwayMap (CC BY-SA).
